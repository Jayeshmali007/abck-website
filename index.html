About Project

Understanding ABCK

A student innovation concept designed around controlled blood collection and temporary storage during emergency situations.

Project Overview

Accidental injuries may cause significant blood loss, making rapid emergency management important. The ABCK concept is designed as a portable system for controlled blood collection and temporary storage in emergency situations.


The project focuses on a blood collection mechanism, collection bag, tubing and essential supporting components, with emphasis on controlled handling and later laboratory evaluation.
